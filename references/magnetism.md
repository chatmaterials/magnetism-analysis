# Magnetism Notes

- Magnetic-state labels here are compact descriptors based on total and local moments; they are not substitutes for a full spin-model analysis.
- Small energy splittings between FM and AFM states should be treated cautiously because they may be within the sensitivity of the chosen functional or cell size.
- Candidate ranking is useful for screening, but exchange couplings, anisotropy, and noncollinear effects still require dedicated calculations.
- Exchange-scale proxies derived from total-energy splittings are useful for triage, but they are not substitutes for a proper mapped spin Hamiltonian.
- Screening mode matters: a ferromagnet-focused ranking and a compensated-order ranking can reasonably prefer different candidates.
